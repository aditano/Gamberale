# Visual character (used to drive the 3D draft)

Compiled from Wikimedia photographs, winter reportage (Lorenzo Scurti / Neve
Appennino), written descriptions of the castle, and the Abruzzo mountain
vernacular. Scene materials are procedural canvas textures, **not** copies of
those photos.

## Skyline

The **white clock-tower** is the village's marker: plastered keep, stone
battlements, circular clocks, a thin **radio antenna**. It sits at the highest
SE end of the spur (Piazza Castello). Terracotta *coppi* roofs pack downhill
toward San Lorenzo. Three sides of the spur drop; woods wrap the rest.

In snow the white tower reads even harder against grey stone and dark fir.

## Palette

- Keep / tower: cream-white plaster over limestone
- Bastions, merlons, quoins, portals: exposed stone
- Ordinary houses: limestone rubble or plaster, 2–3 storeys
- Roofs: aged terracotta coppi, long axis of the lot
- Shutters: green, brown, grey-blue
- Streets: packed stone / cobble, some stepped
- Woods: dark fir, beech, oak
- Distance: Maiella (often snow-capped), Abruzzo National Park, Molise hills

## Not Tuscan

Cooler plaster, greyer stone, tighter packing, higher pitch, more chimneys.
Flower boxes and the odd satellite dish are modern. Windows on the rebuilt
castle are rectangular, not medieval slits.

## Public art (not scanned)

Four fountains by Ananas Kmielauskas (1990s): Atteone in the centre; lupo e
agnello; Alfeo ed Aretusa; Aci / Galatea / Polifemo at Piano d'Ischia. Alpino
monument at Passo della Forcella. v1 places a single stone basin in Piazza San
Lorenzo as a stand-in for Atteone.

## Coat of arms on the scene

Silver field, red freshwater crayfish (*gambero*), not a lobster. Used on the
start-screen mark. Granted 15 May 1957; gonfalone white-and-red, 2 December 1957.
