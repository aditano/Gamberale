# Economy, food, civic life

## Livelihood

Mountain agriculture and a thin tourism season. Famous locally for *patate di
montagna* of the Medio Sangro. Woods of beech, oak and fir hide **Lago di
Gamberale**. Red-deer enclosure (Area Faunistica del Cervo) around 1,500 m.
The comune sits inside **Maiella National Park** and the Comunità montana
Medio Sangro.

Winter: ski on **Monti Pizzi** (~1,600 m) shared with Pizzoferrato. The first
lift in the area was Gamberale's plant at **Passo della Forcella**, closed
since 1999. Valle del Sole is the named tourist locality.

Access: SS 84 from Roccaraso or SS 652 from Castel di Sangro. About 81–109 km
from Chieti depending on the route (Wikipedia EN says 81 km; ItalyHeritage
109 km — different road choices).

## Table

Cheeses, prosciutto, porcini. Typical plates: grilled lamb, carpaccio, wild
boar *porchettato*. Sweets: *pizzelle* filled with cream. The **sagra
dell'agnello** (lamb festival) is in early August; the 2026 edition was
advertised for 7 August on the comune site, alongside a children's show.
Patronal feast of San Lorenzo is 10 August (historically with a sheep sagra).
