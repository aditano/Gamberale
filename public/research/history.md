# History (compressed dossier)

## Name

Attested **Gambarum in Valva** (1063). Etymologies in circulation:

- Germanic *gamhall(os)* “ally” → *Gamalarius*
- The crayfish (*gambero*) on the stemma
- A Lombard condottiero named Gambaro (local tradition, not proven)

Valva is the Roman name tied to Corfinium; the parish later sat in the
diocese of Sulmona-Valva.

## Timeline

- **1063** — Gambarum listed among Norman gains of Goffredo d'Altavilla
  (with Roberto il Guiscardo and Ruggero) driving on Teate / Chieti.
- **1167** — Under William II of Sicily, Raynaldo Gentilis holds Gambarum
  (1 knight) plus nearby fiefs. Catalogus Baronum tradition via Borrelli 1653.
- **12th–13th c.** — Watchtower / castle; a church of San Michele
  (Lombard cult of the Archangel) is later absorbed into the fortress.
  Houses gather around the castle.
- **1320** — Fiscal estimate of the village recorded.
- **15th c.** — Raimondo d'Annecchino (de Anichino).
- **16th c.** — Giovanni Maria d'Annecchino, then Giovanni Crispano.
- Sold to Baron Giuseppe Mellucci (Capua) for 500 ducats; then Marquis
  Odoardo Benedetti (L'Aquila).
- **14 April 1777** — Mascitelli of Atessa, last feudal lords.
- **1706 earthquake** — San Michele inside the castle already in poor repair.
  The inhabited centre drifts downhill toward the Pollice “casino” and the
  church of San Lorenzo, rebuilt **1709**.
- **1881** — Facade inscription: restoration funded by Pasquale Bucci.
- **Early 1900s** — Castle partly ruined; rebuilt in places as a nursery
  for the National Fascist Institute.
- **1943–44, Gustav Line** — Germans occupy the castle as an observation
  post over the Sangro. Civilians evacuated October 1943; some remain.
  Heavy Allied fire (including British 6-inch howitzers from Capracotta on
  24 December 1943) wrecks castle and church. ~200 civilian deaths from
  shells, mines, and shootings (local history: Nunzio Pollice). Germans
  withdraw end of May 1944. **Medaglia d'Argento al Merito Civile**,
  decree 8 September 2005.
- **1984 earthquake** — Guard tower collapses; rebuilt with modern pieces
  in a **pseudo-medieval** language (white plaster, stone merlons). That
  is the silhouette standing today.

## Civic

Mayor (as of 2022–): Maurizio Bucci, lista civica *Insieme per Gamberale*.
Patronal feast 10 August (San Lorenzo), historically with a sheep *sagra*.
Stemma granted 15 May 1957 (DPCM); gonfalone 2 December 1957 (DPR).
