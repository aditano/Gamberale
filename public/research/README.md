# Gamberale research (first draft)

This folder is the source dossier for the 3D recreation. The village is a
real comune in Abruzzo. The 3D scene is a **best-effort first draft**: landmark
footprints and streets come from OpenStreetMap; house packing in the historic
core is densified because OSM only maps ~34 buildings; architecture is
procedural from photographs and written descriptions, not photogrammetry.

## Snapshot (5 September 2026)

- **Comune di Gamberale**, Province of Chieti, Abruzzo, Italy
- Highest comune in the province: **1,343 m** on Monte Sant'Antonio
- Coordinates (village node): **41.90603 N, 14.20732 E**
- Castle: **41.9045 N, 14.2103 E** (OSM way 1433573864, Wikidata Q21196189)
- Church of San Lorenzo Martire: OSM way 1433573854, Wikidata Q3670906
- Population: **260** (31 May 2026, ISTAT / IT Wikipedia); 267 (1 Jan 2026, Tuttitalia)
- Demonym *gamberalesi*. Patron: San Lorenzo, 10 August
- Coat of arms: silver (*d'argento*) with a red crayfish (*gambero di rosso*), DPCM 15 May 1957
- Mayor: Maurizio Bucci, lista civica *Insieme per Gamberale* (from 12 June 2022)
- Neighbors: Ateleta (AQ), Castel del Giudice (IS), Montenerodomo, Palena, Pizzoferrato, Sant'Angelo del Pesco (IS)
- Area 15.56 km². CAP 66040. Dial 0872. ISTAT 069039. Cadastre D898.
- Official site: http://www.comune.gamberale.ch.it/
