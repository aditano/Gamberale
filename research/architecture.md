# Architecture notes used in the 3D draft

## Castello di Gamberale

- OSM way `1433573864`, Wikidata Q21196189, ~176 m² footprint (keep only)
- Written sources describe a **larger** composition than the mapped polygon:
  rectangular body, one side a semicircular **apse**, stone bastions,
  **portico with massive columns**, paved forecourt
- White plastered upper walls against exposed limestone
- Tower: arched openings, **circular clocks**, stone battlements, **radio
  antenna** for the town
- Windows are modern rectangles (post-war / 1984 rebuild)
- Interior murals by Morena Antonucci (not modelled)
- Scene treatment: keep on the OSM footprint, apse to the valley side,
  portico facing the piazza, tower taller than the keep, antenna, clocks

## Chiesa di San Lorenzo Martire

- OSM way `1433573854`, Wikidata Q3670906, ~204 m²
- 18th century, single nave, gable (*struttura a capanna*)
- Campanile against one side
- Rebuilt 1709 after the 1706 earthquake (one source)
- Scene treatment: cream plaster, stone corners, oculus, timber door,
  terracotta roof, side tower with belfry openings

## Ordinary houses

Abruzzo mountain vernacular, not Tuscan colour:

- Limestone rubble or plaster over stone
- 2–3 floors, small shuttered windows, chimney on the ridge
- Terracotta coppi, pitches following the long axis of the lot
- Green, brown, or grey-blue shutters
- Street doors on the downhill side where the spur drops

OSM mapped 34 footprints. Photographs and every written description call
the centre a **packed borgo**. The draft adds procedural houses along
residential / footway / service streets inside a ~95 m radius, skipping
OSM collisions. That is an honest first-draft fill, labelled as such in
the in-app research panel.

## Coat of arms

Silver field, red crayfish. Used on the town-hall shield and the start
screen mark. Not a lobster; a freshwater *gambero*.

## Public art

Four fountains by Lithuanian sculptor Ananas Kmielauskas (1990s): Atteone
(centre), lupo e agnello, Alfeo ed Aretusa, Aci/Galatea/Polifemo (Piano
d'Ischia). Alpino monument at Passo della Forcella. v1 places a single
stone fountain in Piazza San Lorenzo as a stand-in, not a scan of the
Atteone group.
